✉ A letter from my heart

Things I don't always say out loud

Take your time reading this, Amma. Every word is here because you matter to me.

Dear Amma, Sometimes I act like I know everything, and sometimes I forget to show you how grateful I am. I know I can be distracted by games, my laptop, my plans, and the things I dream of doing. I can get impatient, too. But even when I don't say it, I notice the care you give me in so many little ways. Thank you for looking after me, for worrying about me, for helping me when things feel difficult, and for wanting me to have a good future. I might not understand every decision straight away, but I know that your love is behind so many of the things you do. I have big dreams. I want to learn more, build things with technology, do well in my studies, make something of myself, and give my family reasons to feel proud. I know dreams need patience, effort, and responsibility—not just words. I want to keep growing into someone who works hard, treats people kindly, and remembers the people who helped him along the way. And Amma, I don't want you to think that I only come to you when I need something. I want to make more time for you, listen better, help without always being asked, and tell you when something is on my mind. I may still make mistakes. I'm growing and learning, and I need your guidance even when I don't admit it immediately. You don't have to be perfect for me, and I don't have to be perfect for you. We can both have difficult days and still love each other. If I have ever made you feel unappreciated, I'm sorry. I want my actions—not just this website—to show you how much you mean to me. No achievement, college, job, or dream would feel complete if I forgot the people who stood beside me. Whatever I become, I hope I always carry your kindness, strength, and love with me. Thank you for being my Amma. I love you more than these words can explain.
Your child, always ♡
